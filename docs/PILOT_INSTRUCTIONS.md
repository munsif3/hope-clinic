# Practical instructions

1. **Preserve the work.** The deployed changes are still uncommitted locally. Start with implementation prompt 1.
2. **Spend 30 minutes with the doctor.** In the local demo, try fictional cases: find a patient, record treatment, print, book a return, and check history on her phone. Note where she needs help.
3. **Confirm three things:** her preferred language, whether she finishes notes immediately, and whether she tracks medicines dispensed from stock.
4. **Implement in small steps.** Use the prompts individually. Prioritize saved drafts if interruptions are common; prescribing/dispensing separation if stock blocks saving.
5. **Prepare actual use.** Confirm her login, laptop, phone, printer, and a tested backup/restore process. Deploy verified fixes between clinic sessions.
6. **Start small.** Support her through 2–3 sessions. Avoid historical back-entry and indefinite duplicate paperwork. Review the remaining burden after two weeks.

The core app is already deployed. Prove it helps this doctor before adding other clinics or more administration.
